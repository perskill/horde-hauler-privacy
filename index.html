简体中文

本政策由开发者 PikukaLuda 提供，适用于《末路重载 Horde Hauler》包含激励视频广告的 Android 版本，直到本政策更新为止。Windows 版不含广告，也不联网。

1. 游戏本身

游戏没有账号、内购、统计分析或崩溃上报组件。开发者不接收、出售或共享你的个人信息，也不接收下述本机游戏数据。只有你主动点按“看广告”时才会播放激励视频；战斗中不会弹出广告，不看广告也能完整游玩。

2. 广告服务（Google AdMob）

广告由 Google AdMob 提供，使用 Google 移动广告 SDK 与用户消息平台（UMP）。为了投放广告、统计广告效果和防止欺诈，这些组件会自动收集以下数据并发送给 Google：IP 地址（可用于估算大致位置）、与应用和广告的互动（如启动、点按、视频观看）、诊断信息（如加载时间、卡顿与耗电）、设备标识符（Android 广告 ID、应用集 ID）。数据经加密连接（TLS）传输，由 Google 按其政策处理，详见 Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息。

因此含广告的 Android 安装包需要网络权限与广告 ID 权限。你可以在 Android 系统设置中重置或删除广告 ID。

广告 SDK 还在安装包中声明了 Android 的 READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE 普通权限，允许读取蜂窝网络类型、软件版本等基础手机状态；这类权限由系统自动授予，无需运行时授权提示。该权限不等同于 READ_PHONE_STATE，也不授予读取电话号码、通话记录或通讯录的权限。仅凭这项权限声明，不能确定这些基础状态是否被实际读取或上传。

3. 同意与隐私选项

游戏每次启动都会刷新隐私选择状态，并在 Google 要求时显示同意表单（例如欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士）。只有完成所需隐私选择流程且 SDK 允许请求时，游戏才会请求广告。Google 会依据你的选择及适用规则提供个性化、非个性化或有限广告，也可能不投放广告。需要时，可在游戏内“选项 → 隐私选项”中随时更改选择。拒绝或撤回同意不影响游戏其他部分，免费复活与免费重抽始终可用。

4. 保存在设备上的数据

游戏仅在设备上的应用私有存储空间保存当前一局的进度（自动存档及其备份，包括本局是否已领取广告奖励）、研发进度与战绩，以及声音、显示、语言和新手提示设置。这些数据用于继续游戏和保留你的设置，由游戏在本机读取。游戏不访问通讯录、相册、位置、麦克风或其他应用的数据。

Android 版已关闭系统自动备份。上述数据保留在你的设备上，直到你卸载游戏或在系统设置中清除本应用的数据；执行其中任一操作都会删除这些数据。游戏没有服务器账户或云端副本供开发者保留或删除。

5. 外部链接

“素材许可”页中的作者链接只在你主动点击时交给系统浏览器打开。访问外部网站后，该网站可能按其自身隐私政策处理数据。除上述广告服务外，游戏本身不发送网络请求。

6. Android 系统和应用商店

Android 系统及应用商店（例如 Google Play）可能按各自的政策处理安装、崩溃等信息。这些处理不是由游戏接入或控制的；请查阅相应服务的隐私政策。

7. 儿童

游戏面向 13 岁及以上的玩家，不面向儿童，也不参加 Google Play 的“家庭”计划；广告内容等级上限为“青少年”（T）。

8. 政策变更

如果未来版本改变数据处理方式，我们会先更新本政策，并在游戏内“选项 → 关于与许可 → 隐私”展示更新后的说明。

9. 联系开发者

如有隐私问题或请求，请联系 PikukaLuda：perskill888@gmail.com。

English

This policy is provided by PikukaLuda for the Android versions of Horde Hauler that include rewarded video ads, until it is updated. The Windows version has no ads and does not go online.

1. The game itself

The game has no accounts, in-app purchases, analytics or crash reporting. The developer does not receive, sell or share your personal information, and does not receive the local game data described below. A rewarded video plays only when you tap “Watch ad”; ads never pop up during combat, and the whole game can be played without watching any.

2. Ad service (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform (UMP). To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, these components automatically collect the following and send it to Google: IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app and ad interactions (such as launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information (such as load time, hangs and energy use), and device identifiers (the Android advertising ID and app set ID). The data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and handled by Google under its policies; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The Android package with ads therefore requests network access and the advertising ID permission. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android's system settings.

The ad SDK also declares Android's READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE normal permission in the package. It allows access to basic phone state, such as cellular network type and software version, and is granted automatically without a runtime permission prompt. It is distinct from READ_PHONE_STATE and does not grant access to phone numbers, call logs or contacts. The declaration alone does not establish whether this basic state is actually read or uploaded.

3. Consent and privacy options

At each launch, the game refreshes the status of your privacy choices and shows Google's consent form when required (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland). Ads are requested only after any required privacy-choice process is complete and the SDK allows requests. Depending on your choices and applicable requirements, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized or limited ads, or serve no ads. When required, you can change your choice at any time under “Options → Privacy options” in the game. Declining or withdrawing consent does not affect the rest of the game; the free revive and free rerolls are always available.

4. Data stored on your device

The game stores only on your device the progress of your current run (autosave and its backup, including whether this run's ad rewards were claimed), research progress and records, and sound, display, language and tutorial-tip settings. This data is used to continue your game and remember your preferences. It is kept in the app's private storage and read locally by the game. The game does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or other apps' data.

Android system auto-backup is disabled. This data remains on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its app data in system settings; either action deletes it. There is no server account or cloud copy for the developer to retain or delete.

5. External links

Author links on the Credits page open in the system browser only when you tap them. External websites may process data under their own privacy policies. Apart from the ad service above, the game itself sends no network requests.

6. Android and app stores

Android and app stores such as Google Play may process installation, crash and similar information under their own policies. This processing is not integrated into or controlled by the game; please consult the relevant service's privacy policy.

7. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children and is not part of Google Play's Families program; ad content is capped at the Teen (T) rating.

8. Changes

If a future version changes these practices, we will update this policy first and show the revised statement in “Options → About & licences → Privacy” in the game.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact PikukaLuda at perskill888@gmail.com.
