简体中文

本政策由开发者 PikukaLuda 提供，适用于《末路重载 Horde Hauler》0.31.0 及以后版本，直到本政策更新为止。

1. 收集、使用和共享

游戏不收集、上传、出售或共享你的个人信息或设备信息。游戏没有账号、广告、内购、统计分析或崩溃上报组件；Android 安装包不申请网络权限，也不申请其他 Android 权限。开发者不接收或使用下述本机游戏数据，也不向第三方提供这些数据。

2. 保存在设备上的数据

游戏仅在设备上的应用私有存储空间保存当前一局的进度（自动存档及其备份）、研发进度与战绩，以及声音、显示、语言和新手提示设置。这些数据用于继续游戏和保留你的设置，由游戏在本机读取。游戏不访问通讯录、相册、位置、麦克风或其他应用的数据。

Android 版已关闭系统自动备份。上述数据保留在你的设备上，直到你卸载游戏或在系统设置中清除本应用的数据；执行其中任一操作都会删除这些数据。游戏没有服务器账户或云端副本供开发者保留或删除。

3. 外部链接

“素材许可”页中的作者链接只在你主动点击时交给系统浏览器打开。访问外部网站后，该网站可能按其自身隐私政策处理数据；游戏不会向该网站发送你的游戏存档或设置。

4. Android 系统和应用商店

Android 系统及应用商店（例如 Google Play）可能按各自的政策处理安装、崩溃等信息。这些处理不是由游戏接入或控制的；请查阅相应服务的隐私政策。

5. 儿童

游戏不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童的个人信息。

6. 安全与政策变更

本机游戏数据位于 Android 的应用私有存储空间，游戏不把它上传到开发者服务器。如果未来版本改变数据处理方式，我们会先更新本政策，并在游戏内“选项 → 关于与许可 → 隐私”展示更新后的说明。

7. 联系开发者

如有隐私问题或请求，请联系 PikukaLuda：perskill888@gmail.com。

English

This policy is provided by PikukaLuda for Horde Hauler version 0.31.0 and later, until it is updated.

1. Collection, use and sharing

The game does not collect, upload, sell or share your personal or device information. It has no accounts, ads, in-app purchases, analytics or crash reporting. The Android package requests no network permission or other Android permission. The developer does not receive or use the local game data described below and does not provide it to third parties.

2. Data stored on your device

The game stores only on your device the progress of your current run (autosave and its backup), research progress and records, and sound, display, language and tutorial-tip settings. This data is used to continue your game and remember your preferences. It is kept in the app's private storage and read locally by the game. The game does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone or other apps' data.

Android system auto-backup is disabled. This data remains on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its app data in system settings; either action deletes it. There is no server account or cloud copy for the developer to retain or delete.

3. External links

Author links on the Credits page open in the system browser only when you tap them. External websites may process data under their own privacy policies. The game does not send your saves or settings to those websites.

4. Android and app stores

Android and app stores such as Google Play may process installation, crash and similar information under their own policies. This processing is not integrated into or controlled by the game; please consult the relevant service's privacy policy.

5. Children

The game does not collect anyone's personal information, including children's.

6. Security and changes

Local game data is kept in Android app-private storage and is not uploaded to a developer server. If a future version changes these practices, we will update this policy first and show the revised statement in “Options → About & licences → Privacy” in the game.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact PikukaLuda at perskill888@gmail.com.
